How AI fits in

AI is not one step in my process, it runs through all of them. The tools change every few weeks, so I describe the workflow by what I do, not by what I use.

  1. Discover: AI reads and clusters the research, I decide what matters
  2. Define: UX copy, specs and edge cases: AI drafts, I edit
  3. Design: states and variants explored with AI, curated by hand
  4. Build: real components in real code, AI as pair programmer
  5. Verify: AI hunts edge cases and accessibility gaps, I check in the browser
  6. Ship: pull requests into the project's codebase, AI-assisted, reviewed like any other code

Delivery: straight into the project's codebase, not only into Figma. Result: idea → testable prototype, ~3× faster.
