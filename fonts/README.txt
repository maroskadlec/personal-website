These are the three fonts of this computer, kept here so the page does not ask Google for them.

  jersey-10-*.woff2                    Jersey 10 (latin, latin-ext)
  atkinson-hyperlegible-next-*.woff2   Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (variable weight, latin, latin-ext)
  jetbrains-mono-*.woff2               JetBrains Mono (variable weight, latin, latin-ext)

All three are open fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
They are the files Google Fonts serves for these families. Each license text, with the copyright
of its authors, sits next to the fonts, as the license asks:

  OFL-jetbrains-mono.txt               Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors
                                       (https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono)

The texts are the OFL.txt files of the google/fonts repository (github.com/google/fonts, folders
ofl/jersey10, ofl/atkinsonhyperlegiblenext and ofl/jetbrainsmono), downloaded on 2026-10-16 and
not edited. The @font-face rules are in css/fonts.css.
